About this assessment

The GIS Policy Assessment verifies staff knowledge of the school's policy framework for Academic Year 2026–2027: GIS-P01 Child Protection and Safeguarding, GIS-P03 Behaviour and Anti-Bullying, GIS-P05 Digital Safety and Data Protection, and the GIS Teacher Policies.

These policies were developed with reference to the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child, the guidance of the International Task Force on Child Protection (ITFCP), the standards of the Council of International Schools (CIS), UNICEF's child safeguarding guidance and training (AGORA), and the School Curriculum and Standards Authority (SCSA) Teaching, Assessing and Reporting Policy, and are aligned with the laws of the Lao PDR, including the Law on the Protection of the Rights and Interests of Children 2006 (Amended 2023), the Labour Law and the Law on Electronic Data Protection.

The named organisations have not endorsed or partnered with this assessment; they are credited as reference sources. Administered by the Department of the International Curriculum, Golden International School, Vientiane.
